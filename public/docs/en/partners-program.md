We offer a ready-made API for integrating popular messengers — MAX, WhatsApp, Telegram, VK, and Avito — into your CRM, SaaS product, or any other platform. You can expand your product line, increase customer loyalty, and earn from connecting messaging channels.

API documentation is in this section. Apply on the [partners page](https://pact.im/partners-program).

## Available channels

- **MAX** — bot and personal account
- **WhatsApp** — including WABA (WhatsApp Business API)
- **Telegram** — bot and personal account
- **VK** — Direct and communities
- Avito, Odnoklassniki, Instagram

## How the partnership works

You get:

- **A simple, open API** — send and receive messages, automate actions, and run broadcasts. See the [API documentation](/).
- **Ready-made infrastructure** — we run the servers and provide stability, scalability, and protection.
- **A personal chat with technical support** — fast answers to any questions.
- **The ability to set your own price** for end customers (in the Software purchase model).
- **No limits** on conversation volume.

## Three partnership models

In all three models, you build the integration through our API.

| | Software purchase | Partner program 40% | Referral program 20% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer price | You set your own price for connecting and servicing channels | You sell at prices set by Pact | You sell at prices set by Pact |
| Support | You provide full technical support to the customer | You provide full technical support to the customer | We handle technical support for the customer |
| Payments | You accept customer payments yourself | You or we accept payments (your choice) | We accept payments only |
| Billing | You pay monthly for connected channels at wholesale rates | We pay a **40%** commission weekly on all receipts | We pay a **20%** commission weekly on all receipts |

## Why partner with us

- You do not have to figure out how to build messenger connectivity inside your platform — we provide a ready-made solution.
- **High margin** — you set the customer price yourself (in the Software purchase model).
- **Scalability** — connect an unlimited number of channels and customers.
- **Demand** — messenger communication keeps growing, and you offer a product people already want.

## How to become a partner

1. **Apply** on the [partner program page](https://pact.im/partners-program) — a manager will contact you.
2. **Register** — get a partner dashboard with customer and commission stats.
3. **Integrate the API and onboard customers.**
